Article 1

§ 1 General Scope & Exclusivity of Terms

(1) These General Terms and Conditions of Sale and Delivery (Allgemeine Verkaufs- und Lieferbedingungen – AGB) apply exclusively to all current and future business relationships, sales contracts, delivery confirmations, and commercial transactions between Belkern GmbH (hereinafter referred to as the “Seller”) and commercial entities, entrepreneurs (Unternehmer within the meaning of § 14 of the German Civil Code – BGB), legal entities under public law, or special funds under public law (hereinafter referred to as the “Buyer”).

(2) Any diverging, conflicting, or supplementary general terms and conditions of the Buyer shall only become part of the contract if and to the extent that the Seller has expressly consented to their validity in writing. Silence on the part of the Seller or unconditional execution of delivery does not constitute consent.

Article 2

§ 2 Offer, Contract Formation & Quality Specifications

(1) Offers, commodity quotations, and indications issued by the Seller are non-binding (freibleibend) unless explicitly designated as firm offers with a specific acceptance deadline.

(2) A contract is legally concluded upon written confirmation of order (Auftragsbestätigung), sales contract execution, or through formal issuance of a commercial broker slip (Schlussnote) accepted by both parties.

(3) Product specifications (such as coffee screen sizes, defect count, moisture thresholds, piperine content in pepper, or curcumin levels in turmeric) are deemed agreed quality descriptions and not guarantees within the meaning of § 443 BGB unless expressly formulated as an “independent legal guarantee”.

Article 3

§ 3 Pricing, Invoicing & Currencies

(1) Unless otherwise agreed in the individual sales contract, all prices are quoted in Euros (EUR) or US Dollars (USD) net, exclusive of value-added tax (VAT), customs import duties, import handling fees, and European port storage charges (demurrage/detention).

(2) If the agreed delivery term is CIF or CFR European port, ocean freight fluctuations beyond the standard carrier tariff at the date of contract conclusion shall be handled in accordance with the specific Incoterms provisions.

Article 4

§ 4 Delivery Terms, Passing of Risk & Incoterms® 2020

(1) Delivery shall be executed according to the Incoterms® 2020 clause agreed upon in the individual sales contract (principally FOB Hai Phong / Cat Lai, Vietnam; or CFR / CIF major European discharge ports including Hamburg, Rotterdam, Antwerp, or Bremen).

(2) In the case of FOB sales, risk of loss or damage passes to the Buyer as soon as the goods are loaded on board the vessel nominated by the Buyer at the origin port in Vietnam. In the case of CIF/CFR sales, risk of loss passes upon delivery on board the vessel at the port of shipment in accordance with Incoterms® 2020.

Article 5

§ 5 Obligation of Immediate Examination & Notice of Defects (§ 377 HGB)

(1) The Buyer is subject to the statutory examination and notification duties pursuant to § 377 of the German Commercial Code (Handelsgesetzbuch – HGB). The Buyer must inspect delivered container lots immediately upon discharge at the destination port or warehouse.

(2) Any apparent defects, weight discrepancies, damaged container seals, or packaging tears must be reported to the Seller in writing immediately, but no later than five (5) business days following arrival at the final discharge destination. Latent defects must be reported immediately upon discovery.

(3) In the event of a quality dispute, representative joint samples shall be drawn in accordance with GAFTA / European Coffee Federation (ECF) guidelines by an independent certified surveyor (e.g., SGS, Eurofins, or Cafecore).

Article 6

§ 6 Retention of Title (Eigentumsvorbehalt)

(1) Delivered commodities remain the property of the Seller until full payment of all claims arising from the commercial relationship (extended retention of title / verlängerter Eigentumsvorbehalt).

(2) The Buyer is entitled to process, roast, blend, or resell the goods in the ordinary course of business. In such event, the Buyer hereby assigns to the Seller all resulting receivables up to the invoice amount of the Seller’s claim.

Article 7

§ 7 Payment Terms, Letters of Credit & Default

(1) Payment terms shall be agreed in writing in each individual contract (e.g., Irrevocable Letter of Credit confirmed at sight, Cash Against Documents – CAD, or bank transfer within agreed credit periods).

(2) Payments must be effected without deductions directly to the designated bank account of Belkern GmbH in Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

(3) If the Buyer defaults on payment, default interest shall accrue at the statutory rate of nine (9) percentage points above the applicable base interest rate of the Deutsche Bundesbank.

Article 8

§ 8 EUDR & Origin Traceability Compliance

(1) The Seller warrants that all coffee and relevant agricultural products supplied conform to applicable European regulations, specifically Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 on Deforestation-free Products (EUDR), provided such compliance was contracted.

(2) The Seller undertakes to provide the requisite geolocation coordinates, plot-level polygons, and due diligence reference identifiers in a timely manner for customs clearance.

Article 9

§ 9 Force Majeure & Maritime Disruptions

Events of force majeure, including but not limited to typhoons, maritime blockades, war, labor strikes at origin or destination ports, government export embargoes, or epidemic-related shipping disruptions that prevent or unreasonably impede delivery, shall release the Seller from performance obligations for the duration of the hindrance.

Article 10

§ 10 Applicable Law, Place of Jurisdiction & Severability

(1) The laws of the Federal Republic of Germany shall govern all legal relationships between the Seller and Buyer, to the exclusion of the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (CISG).

(2) The exclusive place of jurisdiction for all disputes arising directly or indirectly out of or in connection with the contractual relationship shall be Frankfurt am Main, Germany.

(3) Should any provision of these General Terms and Conditions be or become invalid or unenforceable, the validity of the remaining provisions shall not be affected.
