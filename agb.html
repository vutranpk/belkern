Article 1

§ 1 Scope of Website & Non-Binding Inquiries

(1) This website serves informational purposes for commercial and industrial B2B commodity buyers, coffee roasters, spice processors, and food manufacturers. All technical product specifications, crop indications, harvest calendars, and indicative pricing presented on this website are non-binding (invitatio ad offerendum).

(2) Submitting a Request for Quotation (RFQ) through this website represents an invitation to negotiate and does not constitute a binding commercial sales contract. Legally binding commitments are concluded exclusively through formal written sales contracts executed by authorized commercial representatives.

Article 2

§ 2 Commercial Commodity Contracts Concluded with Origin

(1) Commercial contracts for bulk agricultural commodities (green coffee, steam-sterilized pepper, and agricultural commodities) in Full Container Loads (FCL) are concluded directly between the buyer and the respective processing and exporting entities at origin across Vietnam, Brazil, Indonesia, India, and verified partner origins.

(2) Belkern GmbH operates as the European commercial liaison desk and customer service interface in Frankfurt am Main, facilitating market communication and client support, and does not sell physical commodities on its own book under German domestic sales terms.

Article 3

§ 3 Applicable Trade Association Terms (IGPA & ECC)

(1) In accordance with established international commodity trading practices, commercial sales are subject to standard international trade terms:

  • Spices & Black/White Pepper: Traded under the standard contract rules of the International General Produce Association (IGPA).
  • Green Coffee (Robusta & Arabica): Traded under the terms of the European Coffee Contract (ECC).

(2) Physical commodity trading does not operate under domestic spot sales rules or German statutory commercial defect provisions. Any specific terms or deviations from standard association terms must be explicitly agreed in writing in individual commercial offers and contracts.

Article 4

§ 4 Delivery Terms, Passing of Risk & Incoterms® 2020

(1) Delivery of Full Container Loads (FCL) is executed pursuant to the Incoterms® 2020 clause specified in each individual commercial contract—principally FOB designated origin loading ports or CIF / CFR designated global discharge ports.

(2) Passing of risk, shipping obligations, container demurrage, and marine insurance requirements are governed exclusively by the designated Incoterms® 2020 clause and the applicable IGPA or ECC contract framework.

Article 5

§ 5 Quality Specifications, Sampling & Trade Arbitration

(1) Quality parameters (screen sizing, moisture content, defect count, piperine levels, curcumin content, and pesticide residue limits) are verified prior to shipment by accredited testing laboratories at origin (including ISO/IEC 17025 accredited facilities).

(2) In the event of quality disputes, representative joint samples shall be drawn in accordance with established trade association guidelines (GAFTA, IGPA, or European Coffee Federation). Disputed matters shall be resolved exclusively through the competent commodity arbitration tribunals specified in the governing contract.

Article 6

§ 6 Traceability & EUDR Diligence Documentation

(1) For commodities subject to European Union Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 (EUDR), origin facilities provide plot-level GPS polygon coordinates and due diligence reference identifiers to facilitate customs clearance, where contracted.

(2) Traceability documentation is verified in cooperation with origin farming cooperatives and certification bodies (Rainforest Alliance, 4C) prior to vessel loading.

Article 7

§ 7 European Liaison Desk & Corporate Role of Belkern GmbH

(1) Belkern GmbH is registered at the Amtsgericht Frankfurt am Main under HRB 99468, with registered offices at Melsunger Straße 15, 60389 Frankfurt am Main, Germany (VAT ID: DE 298 174 135).

(2) Belkern GmbH provides customer liaison, technical sample dispatch, documentation coordination, and European time zone (CET) trade communication. Commercial commodity liabilities remain strictly governed by individual contracts concluded directly with origin exporting entities.

Article 8

§ 8 Governing Notice, Intellectual Property & Amendments

(1) The layout, product data sheets, technical literature, and materials on this website are protected under applicable intellectual property laws.

(2) Belkern GmbH reserves the right to update this Commercial Trade Notice as necessary to reflect evolving regulatory frameworks or international trade conditions.
