COMMODITY CUTS & APPLICATIONS

Commercial Processing Forms & Applications.

Highland Arabica coffee selections and preparation styles formulated for specialty roasteries, boutique espresso blends, and premium retail distribution.

Specialty Single Origin

Highland Washed Specialty

Grown in high-altitude volcanic basalt soils with cool climates. Features a vibrant, refined acidity, delicate white floral fragrance, and clean citrus notes, making it an exquisite selection for third-wave specialty roasters.

Roaster Blends

Grade 1 Cleaned Arabica

Wet-milled using fresh highland spring water and carefully machine cleaned. Provides sweet caramel fragrance, balanced body, and subtle chocolate undertones designed to elevate high-end espresso and filter blends.

Artisan Fermentation

Natural Process Micro-Lot

Dried slowly in whole cherry skin on ventilated raised drying beds. The seed absorbs natural fruit sugars, producing a rich, winey cup profile with deep berry sweetness, tropical fruit hints, and a silky smooth body.

Artisan Craft

Yellow Honey Arabica

Pulped with sweet mucilage intact and sun-dried under gentle shade. Delivers an exceptionally sweet, honeyed mouthfeel with pleasant dried apricot and brown sugar aromatics for pour-over and siphon brewing.
