EU DEFORESTATION REGULATION (EUDR) COMPLIANCE

Plot-Level Traceability.
Zero Legal Friction Under EU 2023/1115.

From 2025 onwards, European importers of coffee and agricultural derivatives must satisfy stringent due diligence proof that commodities originate from land that has not experienced deforestation after December 31, 2020. Belkern provides turnkey documentation:

Mandate 01 · Geolocation Plots > 4 ha

GPS Polygon Mapping

Perimeter Coordinate GeoJSON Verification

For plots exceeding 4 hectares, full perimeter coordinate polygon files are mapped and cross-checked against satellite imagery to ensure zero encroachment into primary forest reserves.

Deliverable: GeoJSON / Shapefile Dataset
Mandate 02 · Baseline Dec 31, 2020

Canopy Deforestation Cut-Off

Historical Satellite Dataset Matching

Each physical harvest lot is digitally referenced against historical high-resolution canopy datasets, providing European roasters with conclusive proof of cultivation on established agricultural land.

Deliverable: Canopy Clearance Audit
Mandate 03 · Clearance EU TRACES System

Due Diligence Statements (DDS)

Turnkey European Port Entry Filings

Belkern coordinates directly with European operators to submit required Due Diligence Reference Numbers into the EU Information System prior to vessel customs clearance at European discharge ports.

Deliverable: Official EU Reference Dossier
ORIGIN REGENERATIVE INITIATIVE

Sustainable Pepper Project.
Empowering Farmers, Protecting Soil.

In partnership with the Rainforest Alliance and local agricultural extension centers in Vietnam’s Central Highlands, Phuc Sinh and Belkern pioneered the Sustainable Pepper Program. This initiative transitions traditional monoculture pepper cultivation into an environmentally resilient, biologically diverse agricultural ecosystem:

Soil Health & Chemical Elimination

Eliminating prohibited chemical pesticides, restoring soil microbiome vitality through organic compost, and safeguarding local groundwater tables.

Target Farming Cooperatives

Dak Lak (Cu Kuin, Ea H’leo), Gia Lai, and Ba Ria - Vung Tau smallholder farmer clusters under audited regenerative protocols.

Guaranteed Price Premiums

Direct purchase premiums paid above local market prices, accompanied by continuous agronomy training and personal protective equipment.

European MRL Compliance

European food manufacturers secure certified clean black and white pepper lots with zero glyphosate residue, strictly adhering to EFSA ceilings.
