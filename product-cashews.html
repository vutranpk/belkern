Packaged under clean industrial conditions with automated vacuum evacuation followed by an inert gas backflush (80% N2 / 20% CO2). This lowers residual oxygen to < 1.0%, halting lipid oxidation and preserving fresh nut aromatics during maritime transit across equatorial zones.
