COMMODITY SOURCING & PROCUREMENT

Supplied Strictly Per
Buyer Contract Specifications.

Belkern GmbH sources agricultural commodities directly from established farming cooperatives and processing partners across Vietnam, Brazil, Indonesia, India, and Africa. We do not enforce fixed in-house parameters; every lot is tailored, graded, and certified strictly in accordance with your commercial purchase order and import destination regulations.

Whole & Ground Forms

Supplied in whole bold peppercorns, cleaned black pepper, creamy ivory white peppercorns, and coarse cracked cuts for varied seasoning and manufacturing requirements.

Direct Origin Sourcing & Clean Handling

Harvested from mature perennial pepper vines, naturally sun-cured and processed through spiral gravity separators to remove light berries and organic chaff.

Protective Export Packaging

Packaged in multi-wall Kraft paper sacks with inner moisture liners, polypropylene woven bags, or palletized bulk big bags for containerized ocean shipping.
