02 · Server-Logfiles & Technischer Betrieb

Website Access & Technical Server Log Files

When accessing our website, our web server infrastructure automatically records technical access parameters transmitted by your browser:

  • Browser type and operating system
  • Referrer URL (previously visited website)
  • Date and time of server access
  • IP address of the accessing device (anonymized)

Purpose and Legal Basis: Processing is carried out pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in ensuring the technical stability, network security, and defense against malicious cyber threats. Server log data is kept strictly segregated, never merged with personal identifiers, and automatically purged within 14 days.

03 · Kontaktaufnahme & RFQ-Anfragen

Handling of Contact & RFQ Commodity Inquiries

When contacting us via email, telephone, or through the Request for Quotation (RFQ) desk on our website, the details you submit (name, corporate email, phone, company name, requested commodity specification, container volume, and destination port) are processed exclusively by our commercial desk in Frankfurt am Main.

Purpose and Legal Basis: Data processing is conducted pursuant to Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for responding to commercial inquiries, executing pre-contractual negotiations, and preparing wholesale price indications. Inquiries are stored only for the duration required to process your transaction or as required by statutory commercial record-keeping obligations, after which they are deleted.

04 · Cookies & Notwendige Skripte

Cookies & Essential Web Technologies

Our website uses only technically necessary elements (such as session states for mobile navigation and language preferences). We do not employ third-party tracking cookies, user profiling tools, or marketing pixels.

Statutory Basis per § 25 Abs. 2 TDDDG: The deployment of essential technical scripts and temporary storage on the user's terminal equipment is strictly restricted to providing the telemedia service explicitly requested by the user.
