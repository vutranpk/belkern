02 · Rechtsgrundlagen der Verarbeitung

Data Protection Principles & Legal Bases

We process personal data strictly in compliance with the principles of data minimization, purpose limitation, storage limitation, integrity, and confidentiality (Art. 5 GDPR). Depending on the nature of the transaction, data is processed on the following legal bases:

Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – Contract Initiation

Processing necessary for the performance of a contract or in order to take steps prior to entering into an agreement (RFQ quotation, 500g physical sample dispatch, container booking).

Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR – Legal Obligations

Processing necessary for compliance with legal and statutory duties (tax accounting per § 147 AO, statutory commercial records retention, EUDR polygon records).

Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – Legitimate Interests

Processing necessary for the purposes of legitimate interests pursued by the controller (maintaining IT server security, fraud prevention, B2B wholesale trade operations).

Art. 88 GDPR / § 26 BDSG – HR Employment

Processing necessary for establishing and executing employment relationships pursuant to Section 26 paragraph 1 of the Federal Data Protection Act.

03 · Server-Logfiles & IT-Sicherheit

Web Server Log Files & Technical Operation

When accessing our website, our web server infrastructure automatically collects and stores operational information in so-called server log files, which your browser automatically transmits to us:

  • Browser type and browser version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL (previously visited page)
  • Host name of the accessing device
  • Date and time of server inquiry
  • Internet Protocol (IP) address (truncated/pseudonymized)

This data is not merged with other data sources. The legal basis for data processing is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in the technical administration, cyber defense, bot protection, and ensuring the stability and resilience of our web systems. Server logs are automatically purged after 14 days unless required for forensic evidence in cyber incidents.

04 · Kontaktaufnahme & RFQ-Anfragen

B2B Commodity Inquiries & Quotation Requests

When contacting us via email, telephone, or through the RFQ Procurement Desk on our website (such as contact.html), the information you provide (name, business email, telephone number, company legal name, target commodity, container volume, Incoterm, port of destination, and transaction notes) is stored and processed by our Frankfurt desk.

Purpose and Legal Basis: The processing of this data is carried out pursuant to Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the purpose of answering your inquiry, preparing binding FOB/CIF/CFR wholesale quotations, or conducting pre-contractual negotiations. If no supply contract is established, inquiries are archived or deleted once the commercial inquiry has been concluded, provided no statutory retention duties prevent deletion.

05 · Musterversand & Laborproben

500g Courier Working Sample Dispatches

When requesting physical 500g laboratory working samples (green coffee beans, black/white pepper, spices) for sensory cupping, roasting trials, or third-party laboratory verification, we process the shipping recipient's name, corporate delivery address, and contact telephone number for courier tracking:

Logistics Execution Partners: Physical samples are dispatched via express courier services (e.g., DHL Express, FedEx, UPS). Delivery details are transmitted to these logistics providers strictly for the purpose of parcel delivery and customs declarations pursuant to Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Courier partners process this data in compliance with their own statutory data protection obligations.

06 · EUDR Geodaten & Sorgfaltspflichten

EUDR Polygon Dossiers & Due Diligence

Under Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 (EU Deforestation Regulation – EUDR), importers of coffee and agricultural products must provide geolocation polygon data and due diligence statements for imported commodity batches.

When compliance officers from industrial clients request EUDR GeoJSON polygons, BRCGS audit certificates, or ISO 17025 laboratory test certificates, corporate officer credentials and communication logs are retained pursuant to Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (compliance with EU statutory obligations) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (auditing and chain-of-custody verification).

07 · Bewerbungsverfahren & Personaldaten

Job Applicant Data & Recruitment

When you apply for a position at our Frankfurt desk (as featured on careers.html) via email, we process your curriculum vitae (CV), certificates, contact data, and application correspondence.

Legal Basis and Retention Period: The legal basis is Section 26 paragraph 1 BDSG in conjunction with Art. 88 GDPR. If your application does not result in employment, application files are retained for up to 6 months following formal rejection for the defense of potential legal claims (e.g. under the General Equal Treatment Act – AGG) and deleted thereafter, unless you have granted express consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for talent pool retention.

08 · Cookies & Technische Speicherung

Cookies & Local Browser Technologies

Our website uses strictly necessary technical elements and session parameters to ensure correct display, navigation drawer operation, and language settings. We deliberately avoid intrusive third-party marketing cookies, user profiling algorithms, and cross-site remarketing pixels.

Statutory Notice per § 25 Abs. 2 TDDDG: Storage of information in the terminal equipment of the end user or access to information already stored in the terminal equipment is strictly limited to cases where this is absolutely essential to enable the provider to provide a telemedia service expressly requested by the user (§ 25 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 TDDDG).

11 · Rechte der betroffenen Person

Your Statutory Rights as a Data Subject

Under Chapter III of the GDPR, you have the following rights at any time without incurring any fee beyond standard transmission costs:

Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR)

You have the right to obtain confirmation as to whether your personal data is being processed, and to obtain information regarding purposes and recipients.

Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)

You have the right to request the prompt correction of inaccurate personal data or completion of incomplete records.

Right to Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)

You have the right to obtain erasure of your personal data where statutory grounds apply, provided no statutory retention obligations (such as tax or commercial record retention rules) prevent deletion.

Right to Restriction (Art. 18 GDPR)

You have the right to demand the restriction of processing under certain legal prerequisites.

Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

You have the right to receive personal data you provided in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

Right to Object (Art. 21 GDPR)

You have the right to object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to the processing of personal data based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our compliance desk at mail@belkern.com with the subject “GDPR Data Subject Request”.
