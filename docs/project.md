## 1. Project Positioning (B2B Bulk Export Architecture)

Designing a website for international B2B agricultural commodity export (Bulk Trading) is fundamentally different from a retail shop, café, or consumer specialty spice brand (B2C/Specialty):

* **B2C / Retail:** Sells 250g coffee bags, 50g spice jars. The website emphasizes lifestyle imagery, emotional storytelling, and an "Add to Cart" button.
* **B2B Agricultural Export (Belkern GmbH):** Trades by Full Container Loads (20ft/40ft FCL), measured in Metric Tons (MT).
* **The Commercial Trade Model (German Integrity & Multi-Origin Sourcing Network):**
  * **Belkern GmbH (Frankfurt am Main, Germany):** Independent German legal entity (HRB 99468 Handelsregister Frankfurt, VAT ID: DE 298 174 135, Est. 2014). Commercial contracts governed by German law, zero legal friction, European banking (IBAN DE), and direct coordination in Central European Time (CET).
  * **Global Sourcing Network (Multi-Origin):** Sourced from vetted, established, and reputable commodity producers across Vietnam, Brazil, Indonesia, India, and Africa, delivered strictly per customer contract specifications.

## 2. What Procurement Buyers Need to See (4 Core Pillars)

1. **Capacity, Scale & Direct Origin:**
   * Real industrial processing facilities in Vietnam (eliminating speculative brokers who do not control physical stock).
   * Direct origin pricing, multi-thousand-ton export capacity, and strict quality control from farm to factory.
2. **Strict Technical Standards & EU Compliance:**
   * **EUDR Compliance:** Farm-level GPS polygon traceability verified via **Rainforest Alliance** and **4C** certifications.
   * **Food Safety Standards:** **BRCGS** (Food Safety Certified), **HACCP**, **US FDA**, and industrial **Steam Sterilization** ensuring strict compliance with EU Maximum Residue Limits (**MRLs**) and microbiological safety (Salmonella/E. Coli negative).
   * **Certified Laboratory Results:** Accredited by **VILAS 559 / ILAC-MRA** (Screen 16/18 coffee, 500–570 g/l bulk density pepper, min 5% curcumin turmeric).
3. **Commercial Trade Terms (Incoterms & Export Packaging):**
   * **Incoterms 2020:** FOB (Cat Lai / Hai Phong), CIF & CFR (Hamburg, Rotterdam, Antwerp, Genoa, etc.).
   * **Bulk Packaging:** 60kg Jute bags with GrainPro liners, 1-ton Big Bags (Jumbo), 25/50lbs vacuum cartons/tins (cashews), and multi-ply Kraft/PP bags (spices).
4. **B2B Conversion Funnel (Professional Call-to-Actions):**
   * Strictly no "Buy Now" or "Shopping Cart" buttons.
   * Enterprise B2B actions:
     * `[Request Container FCL Quotation]` / `[Angebot für Container (FCL) anfordern]`
     * `[Request Sample Lot]` / `[Muster anfordern]`
     * `[Download Spec Sheet (PDF)]` / `[Spezifikationsdatenblatt herunterladen]`
