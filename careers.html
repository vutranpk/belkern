Trading Culture & Values

Where European Precision Meets Direct Agricultural Origin.

Belkern GmbH is not a conventional broker. We operate as the European commercial hub directly connecting European industrial buyers with vetted origin processing facilities for green coffee and steam-sterilized pepper. Working here means engaging in tangible, high-volume international trade with short decision loops and genuine commercial autonomy.

Dual Continental Bridge

Collaborate on a daily basis with industrial roasters, spice processors, and beverage multinationals across Europe, while staying intimately connected with vetted origin partners and agronomy teams across primary global origins (Vietnam, Brazil, Indonesia, India, and Africa).

  • Direct factory access in origin countries
  • Bilingual Frankfurt commercial desk

International Trade Standards

Every transaction is governed by established trade rules (Incoterms® 2020, European Coffee Contract, IGPA Terms) and German commercial law. You will gain deep expertise in maritime ocean contracts, letters of credit, and commercial dispute avoidance.

  • Incoterms® 2020 contract governance
  • German Handelsgesetzbuch (HGB) legal certainty

EUDR Compliance & ESG Leadership

Be at the forefront of European supply chain transparency. Belkern leads with polygon geolocation traceability, Rainforest Alliance certification, and laboratory testing (ISO/IEC 17025) ensuring zero deforestation and stringent contaminant safety.

  • EUDR polygon geolocation traceability
  • Rainforest Alliance & 4C certified sourcing
