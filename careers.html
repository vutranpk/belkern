Where European Precision Meets Direct Agricultural Origin.

Belkern GmbH is not a conventional broker. We represent the direct European commercial extension of Phuc Sinh Corporation, Vietnam's leading exporter of coffee and pepper. Working here means engaging in tangible, high-volume international trade with short decision loops and genuine commercial autonomy.

Dual Continental Bridge

Collaborate on a daily basis with commercial buyers, industrial roasters, and spice processors across Europe, while staying intimately connected with dedicated origin processing plants and agronomy teams across Vietnam.

  • Frankfurt am Main trading desk
  • Direct origin factories in Dak Lak & Son La

International Trade Standards

Operate under international commodity standards (IGPA, European Coffee Contract, Incoterms® 2020, ASTA). We prioritize clean governance, EUDR traceability, and rigorous contract execution.

Commodity Logistics & Freight Coordinator (m/f/d)

Governing maritime container supply chains (FCL), port terminal releases, and customs clearance from Vietnam ports to European discharge hubs.

View Details
Core Responsibilities
  • Track ocean container shipments from ports of loading (Cat Lai, Hai Phong) to European discharge ports (Hamburg, Rotterdam, Antwerp).
  • Coordinate import customs clearance, phytosanitary border inspections, and release orders with European customs brokers.
  • Liaise directly with shipping lines (Maersk, MSC, Hapag-Lloyd) on vessel schedules, demurrage monitoring, and booking confirmations.
  • Arrange inland intermodal barge, rail, and trucking transport to European industrial buyer warehouses.
  • Verify ocean freight invoices, terminal handling charges (THC), and demurrage reconciliations against contract terms.
  • Maintain real-time logistics tracking dashboards and advise commercial trading desks on freight rate benchmarks.
Key Qualifications & Skills
  • Professional Background: Vocational qualification as Freight Forwarding Clerk (Kaufmann/-frau für Spedition und Logistikdienstleistung) or degree in Supply Chain / Logistics.
  • Languages: Fluent German (C1/C2) and professional working English (spoken & written) for international carrier communications.
  • Logistics Acumen: Practical experience in maritime sea freight operations, container logistics, and bill of lading (B/L) workflows.
  • Incoterms: Solid understanding of Incoterms® 2020 (FOB, CFR, CIF, DDP) and liability handoffs.
  • Software: High proficiency in Excel for container tracking sheets, demurrage timelines, and freight calculations.
Preferred Experience (An Advantage)
  • Prior experience with agricultural bulk food cargo (green coffee, black pepper, nuts).
  • Familiarity with German customs system ATLAS and European phytosanitary border control posts.
  • Established network with Rhine-Main intermodal transport depots or Hamburg/Rotterdam terminals.
What Belkern Offers
  • Direct operational ownership over high-volume container freight across major global trade lanes.
  • Flat organizational structure with direct lines to executive trade managers and origin port desks.
  • Permanent employment in Frankfurt am Main with competitive market compensation.
  • Modern, ergonomic workplace in Frankfurt-Ost with public transport connectivity.

Leading European compliance dossiers, EUDR polygon traceability, and ISO 17025 laboratory clearance for coffee and spice contracts.

  • Audit and archive EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) polygon geolocation dossiers and due diligence statements.

  • Formulate and maintain Technical Data Sheets (TDS), allergen matrices, and non-GMO certificates for European food processors.
  • Coordinate customer quality audits, sample evaluations, and organic / Rainforest Alliance chain-of-custody documentation.
  • Monitor European food safety legislation (EC 396/2005, EC 2023/915) and advise trading desks on compliance requirements.
  • Serve as the technical QA contact point for European industrial buyers, testing laboratories, and audit bodies.
Key Qualifications & Skills
  • Academic Background: Degree in Food Chemistry, Food Technology, Agricultural Science, Quality Management, or equivalent experience.
  • Languages: Fluent in English (corporate working language) and German (for European client QA dialogues).
  • Standards Knowledge: Good understanding of BRCGS Food Safety, IFS, HACCP Codex Alimentarius, and ISO/IEC 17025.
  • EUDR Acumen: Knowledge or keen interest in EUDR compliance, geolocation polygon validation, and supply chain due diligence.
  • Attention to Detail: Analytical rigor, systematic documentation habits, and high sense of responsibility.
Preferred Experience (An Advantage)
  • Practical experience in green coffee cupping, spice physical analysis, or microbiology testing.
  • Experience with GIS mapping software (QGIS, ArcGIS) or GeoJSON data processing.
  • Familiarity with ASTA cleaning standards or European Coffee Federation contract rules.
What Belkern Offers
  • Pioneering leadership in European EUDR compliance and agricultural sustainability governance.
  • Direct collaboration with accredited origin laboratory facilities (VILAS 559) and agronomy teams.
  • Flexible working model with hybrid office arrangements (Full-Time or Part-Time options).
  • Opportunity to participate in European industry symposiums and food quality forums.
