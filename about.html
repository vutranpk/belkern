CHRONOLOGY OF BILATERAL EXPANSION

From Origin Processing Pioneer to Frankfurt Commercial Gateway.

Phuc Sinh Corporation was founded in 2001 in Ho Chi Minh City with a focused mandate: transforming Vietnam’s agricultural trade from raw commodity brokerage into a value-added, technologically verified industrial export system. Over the past twenty-five years, Phuc Sinh invested heavily into state-of-the-art processing infrastructure, becoming Vietnam’s leading black and white pepper exporter and one of the nation’s top four green coffee exporters.

To eliminate intermediate broker layers and provide European corporate buyers with seamless commercial governance, Belkern GmbH was established in Frankfurt am Main (Commercial Register: HRB 99468). Headquartered in Germany’s central financial and logistics corridor, Belkern provides direct European banking, German-law procurement contracts (HGB), and real-time Central European Time coordination.

Today, Belkern and Phuc Sinh operate as a cohesive bilateral bridge: European roasters and spice processors negotiate and execute supply agreements directly in Frankfurt, while origin factories in Vietnam handle harvest selection, steam sterilization, lab testing, and container loading directly from the port of Hai Phong or Cat Lai.

B2B PROCUREMENT WORKFLOW

From Specification Review
To Vessel Discharge in Europe.

How Belkern and Phuc Sinh execute Full Container Load (FCL) supply contracts with European procurement teams:

01 Intake

Inquiry & Target Specs

The buyer submits commodity specifications (screen size, bulk density, MRL ceilings), container volume (FCL), target delivery window, and discharge port (Hamburg, Rotterdam, Antwerp, Genoa).

02 Matching

Capacity & Lot Reservation

Frankfurt coordinates directly with origin plants in Vietnam to check current crop harvest parameters, verify processing slot schedules, and lock in binding FOB or CIF pricing.

03 Verification

Sample & HGB Contract

A 500g courier working sample is dispatched to the client’s lab. Upon approval, a formal commercial contract is executed under German law by Belkern GmbH.

04 Execution

Loading, Lab & EUDR Dossier

Containers are stuffed under strict quality control. The buyer receives full shipping documents, Bill of Lading, VILAS 559 lab analysis, and EUDR GeoJSON farm polygon files.
